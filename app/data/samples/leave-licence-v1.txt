LEAVE AND LICENCE AGREEMENT

Fictional sample. All names, addresses and numbers in this document are invented.

This Leave and Licence Agreement is made at Pune on 1 April 2026 between Mr. Anil Deshpande, residing at 14 Shivaji Nagar, Pune 411005 (hereinafter referred to as the "Licensor") and Ms. Kavita Rane, residing at 22 Model Colony, Pune 411016 (hereinafter referred to as the "Licensee").

1. PREMISES
1.1 In this agreement, "Premises" means Flat 7B, Sunrise Residency, Baner Road, Pune 411045, admeasuring 620 square feet carpet area, together with one covered parking space.
1.2 The Licensor permits the Licensee to occupy the Premises on leave and licence basis for residential use only.

2. TERM
2.1 This agreement shall commence on 1 May 2026 and shall continue for a period of eleven months, ending on 31 March 2027.
2.2 Renewal shall be at the sole discretion of the Licensor and on such terms as the Licensor may decide at the time of renewal.

3. LICENCE FEE
3.1 The Licensee shall pay a monthly licence fee of Rs. 18,000/- (Rupees Eighteen Thousand only) on or before the fifth day of each calendar month.
3.2 The licence fee shall be increased by 5% on each anniversary of the commencement date.
3.3 If the licence fee is not paid within seven days of the due date, interest at 2% per month shall be payable on the outstanding amount.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT
4.1 The Licensee shall pay an interest free refundable security deposit of Rs. 60,000/- (Rupees Sixty-Five Thousand only) on or before the commencement date.
4.2 The Licensor may deduct from the security deposit any amount towards unpaid licence fees, unpaid utility bills, damage to the Premises, and any other sum the Licensor considers due.
4.3 The balance of the security deposit shall be refunded to the Licensee after the Licensee vacates the Premises and hands over peaceful possession.

5. LOCK-IN PERIOD
5.1 The Licensee shall not vacate the Premises before 31 December 2026. This period shall be the lock-in period.
5.2 If the Licensee vacates the Premises before the end of the lock-in period, the entire security deposit shall stand forfeited to the Licensor.
5.3 The Licensor may terminate this agreement at any time, including during the lock-in period, by giving notice as provided in clause 9.

6. UTILITIES AND MAINTENANCE
6.1 The Licensee shall pay all electricity charges, water charges and internet charges for the Premises as and when they fall due.
6.2 The Licensor shall pay the society maintenance charges and property tax.
6.3 The Licensee shall carry out and pay for all repairs within the Premises, including repairs to plumbing, electrical fittings and appliances, irrespective of the cause.
6.4 The Licensor shall be responsible for structural repairs to the building.

7. USE OF THE PREMISES
7.1 The Licensee shall not sublet, assign or part with possession of the Premises or any part of it.
7.2 The Licensee shall not allow any person other than the Licensee and the Licensee's immediate family to reside in the Premises for more than seven consecutive days without the prior written consent of the Licensor.
7.3 The Licensee shall not keep any pet in the Premises.
7.4 The Licensor or the Licensor's representative may enter the Premises to inspect its condition.

8. SOCIETY RULES
8.1 The Licensee shall observe the rules of Sunrise Residency Co-operative Housing Society and shall be responsible for any fine imposed by the society on account of the Licensee's conduct.

9. TERMINATION
9.1 Either party may terminate this agreement by giving the other party 30 days written notice.
9.2 On termination, the Licensee shall hand over the Premises in the same condition in which it was received, ordinary wear and tear excepted.

10. NOTICE BEFORE VACATING
10.1 The Licensee shall give the Licensor 60 days written notice before vacating the Premises.
10.2 Any notice under this agreement shall be sent to the address of the other party stated above.

11. INDEMNITY
11.1 The Licensee shall indemnify the Licensor against all claims, losses, costs and expenses arising out of the Licensee's use or occupation of the Premises.

12. REGISTRATION
12.1 This agreement shall be registered and the cost of stamp duty and registration shall be borne by the Licensee.

13. DISPUTES
13.1 Any dispute arising out of this agreement shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts at Pune.

SIGNED by the Licensor and the Licensee in the presence of two witnesses on the date first written above.
